Privacy policy

This policy explains how Funvalvar handles information in Nine-Grid Escape for iOS and on this website.

Last updated: 14 October 2026

1. Who we are

Nine-Grid Escape is provided by Funvalvar. For privacy questions or requests, contact funvalvar@proton.me.

2. Local game information

Level progress, the latest game in each level, action history, language, theme, and sound preferences are stored on your device. The app has no account system and does not upload these saves to a Funvalvar-operated server. Deleting the app may remove local information. Device backups are governed by your operating system and backup settings.

3. Advertising and consent services

The iOS app uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). These third-party services may process your IP address and approximate location, app- or device-related identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, product interactions, crash reports, performance information, and other diagnostics. They use this information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, operate consent choices, and maintain their services. Non-personalized ads may still involve data processing.

4. Our advertising configuration

The app requests non-personalized ads, disables Google’s publisher first-party ID feature, and does not request access to the Apple advertising identifier through App Tracking Transparency. This configuration does not mean that Google processes no identifiers or other information. Google’s handling of data is described in its privacy policy and advertising technology disclosures, linked below.

5. Your choices

UMP updates consent status at launch and may display a privacy message where required. Ad requests are made only when the consent service permits them. Where required by that service, More includes Ad privacy settings so you can review or change choices. Gameplay does not require an ad to be available. You may also manage available privacy controls in your device settings.

6. Support emails

If you email us, we receive your email address and the content and attachments you choose to send. We use these to respond and investigate your request. Email is handled through our email provider, Proton. We retain support correspondence only as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations. You can contact us to request deletion, subject to those obligations.

7. This website

This website has no advertising scripts, analytics scripts, contact forms, or third-party font requests. When the site is hosted, its hosting provider may process IP addresses and request details needed to serve and secure pages. We do not use these records to build advertising profiles. Clicking an external link or sending an email is subject to the destination service’s policies.

8. Retention and international processing

Local saves remain on your device until removed. Google and email or hosting providers apply their own retention policies and may process information in other countries. Funvalvar does not control all of their retention periods. See their policies or contact us for questions about information you send directly to us.

9. Children

This app is not marketed as an app specifically for children and is not designated for Apple’s Kids Category. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, please contact us so we can investigate and remove it where appropriate.

10. Requests and updates

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to processing of personal information. Contact funvalvar@proton.me for information handled by Funvalvar. For third-party data, consult the provider’s privacy controls. We may update this policy when our practices change; the date at the top identifies the latest revision.
