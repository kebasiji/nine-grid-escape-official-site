Privacy policy

This policy explains how Funvalvar handles information in NonaGrid for iOS and on this website.

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This policy applies to NonaGrid for iOS, version 1.0.0 and later, and this website.

1. Developer and contact

NonaGrid is provided by Funvalvar. For privacy questions or requests, contact funvalvar@proton.me.

2. Information on your device

Level progress, action history, tutorial state, language, theme and sound preferences are stored on your device. The app has no developer account or developer-operated cloud save service. Funvalvar does not upload these records to its servers. Deleting the app may remove local progress and preferences. Device backups follow your operating system and backup settings.

3. No advertising or tracking

The app does not display advertising, track users across other companies’ apps or websites for advertising, access advertising identifiers, or send gameplay analytics to Funvalvar.

4. Apple in-app purchases

An optional, non-consumable Apple in-app purchase unlocks additional Advanced levels. Apple processes payments under its own privacy policy; Funvalvar does not receive your payment card details. The app uses StoreKit to obtain the product price and verify purchase or Family Sharing entitlements on your device. It does not send receipts, transaction identifiers or purchase history to a Funvalvar server. Purchasing and restoring require a connection to the App Store. Restoring purchases restores access, not deleted local progress. Refunds or revoked sharing may remove access while leaving local game progress intact.

5. Support correspondence

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments. We use them to respond and investigate your request through our email provider, Proton. We retain correspondence only as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations. Contact us to request deletion, subject to those obligations. The app does not automatically upload support information.

6. This website and external services

This website has no advertising or analytics scripts, contact forms, or third-party font requests. Its hosting provider may process IP addresses and request details needed to serve and secure pages. We do not use this information for advertising profiles. Opening a policy or support page uses your browser. Apple, Proton and the hosting provider may process information in other countries and apply their own retention policies.

7. Children and policy updates

The app is not specifically directed at children and is not in the Apple Kids Category. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. Contact funvalvar@proton.me about information you send to Funvalvar, applicable access or deletion rights, or concerns about a child’s information. We update this policy when practices change; the date above identifies the latest revision.
