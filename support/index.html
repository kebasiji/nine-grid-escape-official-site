Learn the controls, understand the board, or get in touch with Funvalvar.

How to play

Move

Tap an adjacent cell. Ordinary actions cost energy and rotate the outer ring clockwise.

Rotate

Tap the robot or Rotate CW to spend 1 energy rotating the outer ring one tile clockwise. Use Rotate CCW once per level for a free counterclockwise turn. Rotation leaves the robot, key, and exit in place and does not activate facilities under the robot.

Finish a stage

Collect the key and reach the exit with energy remaining in every stage. Reaching it at zero energy still means failure. Getting started levels have 1 stage; Advanced challenges have 3, with energy, your shield, and any unused reverse turn carried over.

Undo or restart

Undo as often as needed within the current stage, including after failure; it restores the resources and reverse use spent by that action. Restart Stage restores the board and resources you entered with. Restart Level begins at stage 1 with full resources and asks for confirmation if you have made progress. Single-stage levels offer only Restart Level. Cleared status and unlocked levels are preserved.

Frequently asked questions

How do I unlock levels?

There are 4 Getting started levels and 23 Advanced challenges. Level 1 of both paths is available from the start, and later levels unlock in order within each path. Getting started levels have 1 stage each and Advanced challenges have 3: 73 puzzle boards in total, plus a separate tutorial.

How does the tutorial work?

Learn movement, clockwise rotation, and collecting the key in 3 guided actions, then find your own way to the exit. You can start with the tutorial or go straight to level selection. Revisit it from More and return to your previous game afterward.

Can I play offline?

Yes. The puzzle content and progress are stored on your device. iOS ads and privacy messages use the internet, but unavailable ads do not block play.

Will my progress move to another device?

This version has local saves and no developer-operated cloud sync. Switching levels preserves each level’s latest game. Deleting the app may remove its progress and preferences; operating-system backups may behave differently.

What does a shield do?

Entering an electric grid or gate with a shield uses the shield and costs one energy in total. Without a shield, an electric grid costs three energy and a gate cannot be entered.

How do charging cells work?

When resources are not full, entering an unused charging cell costs one energy, then adds two energy up to 12 and restores your shield. After activation, it moves with its facility and acts as a path on later visits. If you start the action with full energy and a shield, the cell remains unused. A facility rotating under the robot, clockwise rotation, and reversal do not activate a charger.

Can I change language, theme, or sound?

Open More. The language, theme, and sound settings do not change your puzzle progress. During the tutorial, use the visible language shortcut.

When do ads appear?

The iOS app only attempts an interstitial after an eligible Advanced level completion. The tutorial, Getting started levels, Advanced level 1, failures, retries, and stage changes show none. The first ad requires 8 minutes of active play and at least 2 different Advanced level completions. Later ads require another 6 minutes of active play after the previous ad closes and at least 2 more different Advanced level completions. Limits are 3 per session and 6 per device per local calendar day; each Advanced level counts toward the completion requirement once per day. Active play counts foreground board observation and actions, excluding menus, background time, ads, failure, and results screens. Timing and daily history are stored locally across restarts. If an ad is unavailable or fails, play continues without waiting or showing it later.

Where can I change ad privacy choices?

When Google’s consent service requires this option, More includes Ad privacy settings. Availability depends on region and consent status.

Are there purchases or accounts?

This version has no login, in-app purchases, or subscriptions.
