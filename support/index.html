A little help for your next escape.

Learn the controls, understand the board, or get in touch with Funvalvar.

How to play

Move

Tap an adjacent cell. Ordinary actions cost energy and rotate the outer ring clockwise.

Rotate

Tap the robot or Rotate CW to spend 1 energy rotating the outer ring one tile clockwise. Use Rotate CCW once per level for a free counterclockwise turn. Rotation leaves the robot, key, and exit in place and does not activate facilities under the robot.

Finish a stage

Collect the key and reach the exit with energy remaining in every stage. Reaching it at zero energy still means failure. Beginner levels have 1 stage; Advanced challenges have 3, with energy, your shield, and any unused reverse turn carried over.

Undo or restart

Undo as often as needed within the current stage, including after failure; it restores the resources and reverse use spent by that action. Restart Stage restores the board and resources you entered with. Restart Level begins at stage 1 with full resources and asks for confirmation if you have made progress. Single-stage levels offer only Restart Level. Cleared status and unlocked levels are preserved.

Frequently asked questions

How do I unlock levels?

For iOS: four tutorials, all six Beginner levels, and Advanced level 1 are free. One non-consumable purchase unlocks Advanced levels 2–27: 26 three-stage levels and 78 boards, played in order. No ads, subscriptions or developer account. Family Sharing and Restore Purchases are supported. Purchased puzzles work offline; purchases and restoration need the App Store. Other platform editions may offer different access.

How does the tutorial work?

Choose Basic tutorial, Gates and shields, Electric grids and shields, or Rotation practice. Every tutorial starts with fresh resources and is directly available. During a tutorial, open Gameplay tutorials at the top to switch; during a normal level, open it from More. Return to your original game with its progress preserved. Unfinished optional tutorials retain their place when switching in the same session and restart after relaunch.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Free and already-purchased puzzles work offline. Buying, fetching prices or restoring purchases requires a connection to the App Store.

Will my progress move to another device?

This version has local saves and no developer-operated cloud sync. Switching levels preserves each level’s latest game. Deleting the app may remove its progress and preferences; operating-system backups may behave differently.

What does a shield do?

Entering an electric grid or gate with a shield uses the shield and costs one energy in total. Without a shield, an electric grid costs three energy and a gate cannot be entered.

How do charging cells work?

When resources are not full, entering an unused charging cell costs one energy, then adds two energy up to 12 and restores your shield. After activation, it moves with its facility and acts as a path on later visits. If you start the action with full energy and a shield, the cell remains unused. A facility rotating under the robot, clockwise rotation, and reversal do not activate a charger.

Can I change language, theme, or sound?

Open More to choose language, theme, or sound without changing your normal game progress. More is also available during tutorials; this version does not use a separate tutorial language shortcut.

Are there ads or subscriptions?

The iOS version 1.0.0 has no ads or subscriptions.

How do I purchase or restore access?

Open More → Unlock full campaign, or select a paid Advanced level. Restore purchases is available in More and on the purchase screen. Apple supplies the localized price. No developer account is needed. Family Sharing is supported.

What if I reinstall, request a refund, or lose sharing access?

Restoring a purchase restores access, not deleted local progress. An eligible Apple Account or Family Sharing entitlement can restore access on another device; local game saves do not sync. Refunds or revoked sharing may lock paid levels again without deleting their saved progress.
